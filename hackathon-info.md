## The challenge

Build a personal agent that helps you or your team with something in your personal life. It can do one thing really well or help across several parts of your day. Using co-host tools is welcome but not required.

## Notes from the sponsor talks

- Mastra is a TypeScript framework, so the agent side is easiest in TypeScript. Assistant UI is React and integrates with Mastra.
- Fly sprites are persistent Linux computers that stay on between sessions and have connectors for services like Neon. They do not expire after 24 hours like many sandboxes.
- Exa also offers an agent product that runs searches in bulk. A minimal search tier is priced per query.
- Kernel browsers start in about 30 milliseconds and can stay signed in to sites.
- AgentMail gives agents their own inboxes and works with a custom domain.
- CodeRabbit reviews code and has a pledge to open source; the $10k side quest is separate from it.
